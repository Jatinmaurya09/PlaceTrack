💡 How to Improve Your ATS Score

1
Add role-specific keywords
For Data roles: Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Tableau, Statistics. For Web: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Flask, REST API.
2
Quantify your achievements
Instead of "Built a website", write "Built a website serving 500+ users with 95% uptime".
3
List technical skills clearly
Add a dedicated "Skills" section. Don't hide keywords inside paragraphs — ATS systems scan for them.
4
Include soft skills too
Keywords like "communication", "teamwork", "leadership", "problem solving" also count.
5
Tailor for each role
Keep 2-3 versions of your resume — one for each role you're applying to.
